# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Harness Bridge project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or level of experience.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Being respectful, empathetic, and constructive in discussions and code reviews.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and project quality.
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Giving credit where credit is due.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, discriminatory language, or derogatory comments.
- Trolling, insulting remarks, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate corrective action in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate or harmful.

To report violations, please contact the maintainer at `sukumar.rtg@gmail.com`. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
